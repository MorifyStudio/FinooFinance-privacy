1. Summary

Finoo Finance (the “App”) is a privacy-first mobile app for personal budgeting and expense tracking.

• There is no bank-account linking; we never ask for online banking credentials.
• Your financial data is stored on your device by default (local-first).
• We do not sell your data to third parties for advertising or profiling.
• On Pro, optional backups can be saved as encrypted files to your own cloud accounts (e.g. iCloud / Google Drive)—not on an advertising backend we control.

This policy explains what data is processed, why, and your rights.

2. Data controller

App operator: Morify Studio

Contact: morifystudio@gmail.com

We act as the data controller (or equivalent) under applicable law. Publisher details shown on the app store listing also apply.

3. Data we process

We may process the following data as needed to run the App:

A) Financial records you enter
• Account names and balances, income/expense transactions, categories, notes
• Debt, loan, credit-card, bill, subscription, and goal information
• Vault / holdings amounts (FX, gold, crypto, etc.)
• Optional receipt images (when you pick them from your device)

B) App preferences
• Language, theme, typeface, privacy mode, notification preferences
• Biometric lock setting (Face ID / fingerprint via on-device APIs only; biometric templates are not sent to us)

C) Limited technical data
• Device model and OS version for crash/debug purposes (if an analytics tool is enabled and permitted)
• Public market-rate requests over the internet for FX/gold (your personal transaction history is not attached to those requests)

D) Purchases (Pro)
• Subscription status; payments run through App Store / Google Play. We do not store your card number.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Data is processed to:

• Provide budgeting, net-worth, reporting, and reminder features (performance of a contract / providing the service)
• Maintain security and fix bugs (legitimate interests)
• Verify Pro subscription status (performance of a contract)
• Comply with legal obligations (legal obligation)

We do not process data to build advertising profiles or to sell your information.

5. Storage and retention

• Default storage: local storage on your device (app sandbox).
• Backups: Your ledger is not uploaded to our servers until you use export or personal-cloud backup features.
• Retention: Data remains on device while you use the App and do not delete it. Using in-app delete or uninstalling may remove local data (subject to OS app-data rules).
• Store purchase records: governed by Apple / Google retention policies.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or rent your financial records to ad networks or data brokers.

Limited third parties may be involved:

• App stores (Apple / Google) — distribution and purchases
• Device OS — biometric authentication and local notifications
• Market data providers — general rate information (your personal ledger is not shared)
• Shares you initiate — e.g. sending a CSV/PDF/JSON backup via email or Drive yourself

We will not disclose your financial content to third parties except where legally required (e.g. valid court order).

7. Security

• Local data is protected by the device app sandbox.
• Optional app lock (Face ID / fingerprint / device authentication) is supported.
• Privacy mode lets you mask amounts on screen.
• Pro backups are designed to be encrypted; you decide who receives any backup file.

No system is 100% secure. Keep your device updated and store backups safely.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (e.g. GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to:

• Access and receive a copy of your data (export)
• Correct and delete data (in-app edit / account deletion)
• Restrict or object to certain processing
• Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Contact: morifystudio@gmail.com

Most of your data already lives on your device; export and deletion tools are provided in the App.

10. International transfers

Your local ledger stays on your device. Store subscription checks or optional cloud backups may involve systems outside your region. In those cases, the relevant provider policies (Apple, Google, etc.) apply.

11. Changes
